# Good Day (GD)

**Mindful recipes for a good day.**

Good Day (GD) is a Claude connector for the person who plans a household's
meals. Tell it who eats at your table — including the one who refuses half of
what you cook — and how much time you really have at the stove, and it helps
you plan a week of dinners everyone will eat, build the shopping list, see what
each evening costs you, and work out what you can cook tonight from what's
already in the kitchen.

- **One dish for the whole table.** Describe a picky eater once and GD stops
  proposing anything they won't eat — no second meal for anybody.
- **A week you can actually cook.** Every dish shows its hands-on minutes
  against your daily budget, so a plan never asks for more evening than you have.
- **Plan → list → days.** Finalizing a plan builds the grocery list and puts
  the meals on real dates, alongside tasks like "thaw the chicken".
- **Tonight, from the pantry.** Ask what you can cook now and get an answer
  ranked by how little is missing.

Every recipe is first-party: hand-curated by the GD team or generated by GD's own
offline pipeline, grounded in public-domain reference data (USDA nutrition facts,
FDA food-safety temperatures). Nothing is scraped or copied from third-party
recipe sites, and each recipe carries provenance and trust labels.

GD keeps your household's kitchen data under an account you sign in to. It
stores **no child-identifying data** — household members are described by a
role you choose ("kid 1"), never a name, age or birthdate. You can delete your
account and everything in it from inside Claude at any time. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Setup

### Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop, mobile)

1. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Add custom connector**.
3. Enter the URL: `https://gd.fly.dev/mcp`
4. Click **Add**, then **Connect**. A sign-in page opens: create a Good Day
   account or sign in, and approve access.

### Claude Code

```
claude mcp add --transport http gd https://gd.fly.dev/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp`, choose `gd`, and complete the sign-in in your browser.

Then just ask Claude things like *"Plan our dinners this week — one of the
kids won't eat anything spicy"* — see [worked examples](examples.md).
