# Good Day (GD)

**Mindful recipes for a good day.**

Good Day (GD) is a Claude connector that gives Claude access to a curated and
AI-crafted recipe catalog. In v1 it is **unauthenticated and read-only**: you can
search the catalog in natural language, browse with filters, view full recipes
(ingredients, steps, times, servings), and — when the catalog doesn't have what
you want — log a recipe request so a future catalog batch can fill the gap.

Every recipe is first-party: hand-curated by the GD team or generated by GD's own
offline pipeline, grounded in public-domain reference data (USDA nutrition facts,
FDA food-safety temperatures). Nothing is scraped or copied from third-party
recipe sites, and each recipe carries honest provenance and trust labels.

## Setup

### Claude (claude.ai)

1. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Add custom connector**.
3. Enter the URL: `https://gd.fly.dev/mcp`
4. Click **Add**. No login or API key is needed.

### Claude Desktop

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** (make sure you're on a recent version of Claude Desktop).
2. Click **Add custom connector** and enter `https://gd.fly.dev/mcp`.
3. Save — the Good Day (GD) tools will appear in the tools menu for new chats.

Then just ask Claude things like *"Find me a quick vegan dinner"* — see
[worked examples](examples.md).
