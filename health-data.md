# Consumer health data notice

**Good Day (GD) — Claude connector**

**Effective date: _to be set on the day v2 goes live_**

This notice supplements our [Privacy policy](privacy.md). It explains how GD
handles **consumer health data** as defined by Washington's My Health My Data
Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, and applies to residents of those
states. GD is operated by **_[legal name of the business]_**.

## What health data we collect

GD does not ask about medical conditions. But some of what you tell GD to plan
meals could reveal something about a person's health:

- **Ingredients to exclude**, which may include allergens (for example
  "peanuts" or "shellfish").
- **Diet type** (vegan, vegetarian or non-vegetarian), where it reflects a
  health need.
- **Household members' dislikes and notes**, if you write a health reason into
  them (for example "no gluten"). These are free text; please don't add health
  details that GD doesn't need.

## Where it comes from

Only from you, through the requests Claude sends to GD on your behalf. We
collect it only when you choose to enter it. GD does not buy health data,
infer health conditions, or collect it from other sources.

## Why we use it

Only to keep excluded ingredients out of your meal plans, recipe suggestions
and grocery lists — the reason you gave it to us. We do not use it for
advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models.

## Who we share it with

- **Neon** (database) and **Fly.io** (application hosting), which store and
  process it on our behalf to run GD. WorkOS, our sign-in provider, does not
  receive it.
- **Claude**, when GD answers a request you made, so Claude can respond to you.

We do **not sell** consumer health data, and we do not share it with any other
third party except when the law requires it.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Confirm and access** — find out whether we hold your consumer health data
  and see it, including a list of the parties it is shared with.
- **Delete** — have it deleted, including from our providers' systems.
- **Withdraw consent** — stop GD from collecting or using it.

The quickest way to see, change or delete this data is to ask Claude — for
example *"show my Good Day profile"*, *"remove peanuts from my exclusions"* or
*"delete my Good Day account"*. You can also email
[founder@gooddayplan.com](mailto:founder@gooddayplan.com). We will respond
within 45 days. If we decline your request, reply to our answer to appeal, and
we will respond to the appeal within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you may
contact your state Attorney General.
